shihab_app

shihab_app is a private, personal assistant tool used only by its owner to read and organize the owner's own Gmail and Google Calendar. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Privacy Policy

Who uses it. Only the owner's own Google account signs in to this app.

What data it accesses. With the owner's permission, the app reads, labels and drafts emails in the owner's Gmail, and reads and manages the owner's Google Calendar.

How data is used. Data is used only to provide the owner's personal assistant features. It is processed on the owner's own computer and the services the owner chooses to use. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.

Storage and security. Sign-in tokens are stored privately on the owner's computer. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API data. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
